Distributions of physiological measurement in critically ill children, from two published studies. Mirrored here so they remain available; each figure runs entirely in your browser.

Cuff against invasive blood pressure

Paired oscillometric and intra-arterial readings, by age group and pressure type: what an arterial line would have shown for a given cuff reading.

Goodwin A, Mazwi ML, Somer J, Schwartz SM, McEwan A, Eytan D. Blood pressure in critically ill children: exploratory analyses of concurrent invasive and noninvasive measurements. Crit Care Explor 2021;3(11):e0586.

Oxygen dissociation

Measured distributions of blood-gas oxygen tension against saturation, with the effect of age, pH and carbon dioxide, compared with the classic curve.
